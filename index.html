Your visit to Skansen

Bring your wedding invitation to the entrance. If you do not have a printed invitation, give our full names — Argyrios Kerezis and Tomislav Posavac — together with Swedenborgs lusthus and the confirmed ceremony time.

You are welcome to enter Skansen from 10:00 on the wedding day. Access to the ceremony area begins no earlier than 15 minutes before the ceremony.

Guests attending will receive a Swedish entrance card by email once the ceremony time is confirmed. The accompanying email will be in the language you select when replying.
