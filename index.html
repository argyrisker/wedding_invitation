Dear friends and family

Our journey together has led us here. We are delighted to invite you to our wedding as we say ‘yes’ to each other at Stockholm City Hall, beneath the three golden crowns and beside the waters of Lake Mälaren. One of us grew up in Serres in northern Greece, the other in Virovitica in Slavonia. Two inland towns far apart, and here in the north we have found our shared home. It would mean the world to us to celebrate this day with you.
